What problems does TCS Research study?

These examples were selected from published records in the screened research collection. They describe the questions the papers investigate. The titles alone cannot establish deployment or business impact.

How can clinicians interpret patient measurements?

Predict disease progression from hospital data and estimate blood pressure from cuff pressure and sound.

  • A Data-Driven Approach to Predicting and Visualizing Disease Progression in Hospitalized Patients (2025)
  • Accurate Blood Pressure Prediction using Korotkoff Sound and Cuff Pressure: A Pilot Study (2025)

Can language models assist research reliably?

Evaluate the design of ablation studies and test whether persona agents recognize the limits of their knowledge.

  • AbGen: Evaluating Large Language Models in Ablation Study Design and Evaluation for Scientific Research (2025)
  • Know Thyself: Validating Knowledge Awareness of LLM-based Persona Agents (2025)

Where should AI workloads run?

Choose how to place workloads across edge and cloud systems and estimate inference latency on cloud accelerators.

  • CADAEC: Content-Aware Deployment of AI Workloads in Edge-Cloud Ecosystem (2025)
  • A Comparative Study of ML Models for Predicting LLM Inference Latency on Cloud Accelerators (2025)

How can researchers design materials and energy systems?

Explore alloy compositions and processing routes with multi-agent learning, and optimize solid-state battery designs using physics and machine learning.

  • A multi-agent deep reinforcement learning framework for the generative design of alloys and processing routes (2026)
  • A Hybrid Physics Based-ML Model for Optimization of Solid-State Battery Design (2025)

How can operators detect changes or faults?

Detect land-surface changes in optical and radar imagery and identify pipeline leaks from acoustic signals.

  • A Deep Learning Framework using Remote-Sensing Foundation Model with Siamese U-Net and KAN-Based CNN for Optical and SAR Change Detection (2025)
  • A Data-Driven Approach to Leak Identification and Severity Analysis in Pipelines Using Acoustic Sensing and Deep Learning (2025)

How can industrial and public infrastructure be operated more efficiently?

Study sorting-terminal operations and simulate water-management strategies using a digital twin.

  • Improving Operational Efficiency of Sorting Terminals in Logistics Industry (2025)
  • A Digital Twin of Water Network for Exploring Sustainable Water Management Strategies (2025)

How can robots plan and execute physical tasks?

Study motion planning and control of a drone carrying a suspended load.

  • GPD: Guided Polynomial Diffusion for Motion Planning (2025)
  • Data Driven Model Predictive Control of a UAV Carrying a Cable Suspended Load (2025)

How can enterprises modernize software and protect data?

Study decomposition into domain-aligned microservices and privacy-preserving cloud data sharing.

  • Domain Aligned Microservices Decomposition (2025)
  • Efficient privacy enabled data sharing in cloud using a novel Homomorphic Proxy Re-Encryption Scheme (2025)

What can quantum methods contribute?

Explore how to prepare molecular ground states on quantum computers and how enterprises could migrate devices to post-quantum cryptography.

  • Efficient Preparation of Molecular Ground States on Quantum Computers with Givens Rotations (2025)

Where the publication mix is moving

Compare research domains

Select a year to inspect its counts.

Language models gained share throughout the window. On the same calendar dates each year, NLP/LLM accounted for 8.5%, 13.8%, 15.0%, 16.4% in chronological order. A separate title check for "large language model", "LLM" or "LLMs" gave 0.8%, 5.8%, 8.9%, 9.8% of all eligible works. Among published works alone, the NLP/LLM share was nearly flat between 2025 (15.0%) and 2026 (15.1%). The recent increase is more visible when preprints are included.

Agent research rose most recently. The comparable yearly counts were 2, 6, 5, 13. Of the 13 in the last window, 6 were marked published. The category includes multi-agent methods as well as language-model agents.

The shift spans several fields. Edge/Systems accounted for 14.6%, 10.1%, 8.6%, 5.2% across the four windows. Quantum accounted for 1.4%, 2.8%, 4.0%, 4.9%. CV/VLM combines computer vision and vision-language research, so its total cannot isolate multimodal model growth.

Method

How the list was built

Discovery combined OpenAlex institution and affiliation searches, author snowballing, DBLP via SPARQL, Crossref, and arXiv. The table shows new works at each pass, before final deduplication and filtering.

TCS evidence. The dataset checks whether an author’s own paper affiliation names TCS. The clean subset additionally requires a TCS Research, TCS Innovation, TRDDC or Tata Research Development affiliation, or a match to the dataset’s core TCS author set, and applies the dataset’s clean inclusion rules.

Deduplication. Discovery resolves records by OpenAlex ID, DOI, arXiv ID, and fuzzy title. The published version is retained when available, with its arXiv link preserved.

Venue tiers. A manually curated list takes precedence over CORE 2023 conference ranks. Workshops are treated separately, and qualifying ACL, EMNLP and NAACL Findings are included as A (Findings).

Author profiles. Profiles require TCS-affiliated works. The dataset merges profiles using matching identifiers or corroborating name and coauthor evidence, with manual overrides.

Spot checks. Across 40 sampled rows, source checks confirmed 33, contradicted 0, and could not verify 7.

Limitations

  • OpenAlex coverage depends on available and parsed affiliations and author profiles.
  • 838 candidates lacked sufficient affiliation evidence and were left out of the publication counts.
  • Journal coverage has no SJR ranks. Journal tiers rely on the manual list.
  • The publication mix describes indexed outputs. Internal projects, investment and staffing require separate evidence.
  • Keyword-based domains and focus lists can miss relevant work and include incidental matches.
  • The checker has limited access to affiliations on some publisher pages, including ACL Anthology, Elsevier and SPIE.
  • Absence of a paper ≠ absence of work.
