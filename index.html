About

I'm a full-time PhD research scholar at Manipal Institute of Technology Bengaluru, working with Dr. Sanket Mishra on device-free sensing with WiFi Channel State Information.

I came to research from security practice. I've worked as a cybersecurity engineer, ethical hacker and corporate trainer, and I hold the Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) credential. My M.Tech at KLS Gogte Institute of Technology (CGPA 9.13, First Class with Distinction) produced KubeMisconfigBench, a benchmark for prioritising Kubernetes misconfigurations by real-world context.

I care about research that survives contact with real deployments: noisy signals, messy clusters and cheap hardware.

Research

Device-free wildlife monitoring with WiFi CSI

Cameras and tags are expensive, intrusive and limited by light and line of sight. WiFi signals pass through foliage and darkness, and the way their channel state shifts reveals movement nearby. My PhD asks how reliably commodity WiFi can detect and characterise animal presence without anything attached to the animal.
