# jatasyaprashant.github.io

Personal website of Prashant Badiger, PhD research scholar at MIT Bengaluru (MAHE).

Live at https://jatasyaprashant.github.io

## Before publishing
- Search `index.html` for `YOUR_` and fill in email, LinkedIn, Google Scholar, ORCID, years and B.E. details.
- Add `photo.jpg` (square, at least 400x400) and `cv.pdf` to this folder.
- To show the photo, replace `<div class="portrait">PB</div>` with
  `<div class="portrait"><img src="photo.jpg" alt="Prashant Badiger"></div>`.
